Tic-Tac-Toe Theme - Canonical Take

Purpose:
Preserve the founder-approved original Tic-Tac-Toe background music as reusable project assets.

Files:
- ttt-theme-canonical-take.wav: archival 16-bit stereo WAV.
- ttt-theme-canonical-take.mp3: portable 192 kbps MP3.
- ttt-theme-canonical-take.recipe.json: source recipe, seed, chord pools, melody events, and render metadata.

Source:
The live game music is generated in index.html with WebAudio. It uses a 74 BPM C-G-Am-F progression, two bars per chord, harp-like plucks, soft bass pad, and procedural melody notes. This asset freezes one canonical take from that music design so it can be reused outside the game.

Important:
Do not delete or replace these files casually. This is an early One Day Games / Tic-Tac-Toe creative artifact that CJ directed and approved.
